# Brag Plan: Weaver

## Planning rubric
1. App: a journal and note taker that quietly connects what you write.
2. Strongest claim: people, memories, and useful next steps emerge from your entries (README).
3. Visual hook: a note turns into source-backed memories in the warm sage workspace.
4. Actual UI: Journal composer, People > About, For you suggestion with source link.
5. Shortest satisfying film: 22 seconds.
6. Tone: polished; a quiet editorial product film, tactile paper and deliberate motion.
7. Audio: warm music bed and two soft motion-matched accents; no narration.
8. Caption: Built Weaver: a journal that connects your notes to the people, memories, and next steps inside them.
9. User flow: write a note → save → view a connected profile and follow-up.

## Angle and hook
“A place for your days.” Start with the actual composer, already holding a small ordinary moment. Show what that moment becomes, rather than listing capabilities.
