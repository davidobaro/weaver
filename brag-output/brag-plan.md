# Weaver — Write it once

Weaver is a journal and note taker that quietly connects what you write. It helps people remember their days, the people in them, and their own loose ends without filing everything manually.

Angle: passive, source-backed organization. Strongest claim shown: one journal entry becomes a person's quick fact and a personal task; a later entry can mark that task done. Visual hook: oversized editorial type and the actual woven mark, opening into the real journal composer. Tone: warm, restrained, polished. Caption: Write a little. Weaver connects the details.

Identity: actual DM Sans, Manrope, and Georgia. Cream #f7f6f2, sidebar #eeefe7, evergreen #3b553f. Reuse App, ForYou, Wordmark, WovenThreads and the stylesheet in an isolated film build. Adapters seed fictional data and expose a deterministic timeline. The application itself stays untouched. Focus crops enlarge the actual UI.

All names and journal content are fictional demonstration fixtures. No database, private notes, credentials or private screenshots are used. Prepared states illustrate existing behavior without a live model call. No public availability or external notification claims.
