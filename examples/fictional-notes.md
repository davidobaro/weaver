# Fictional demo notes

Use these in the app. Each submission makes a real call to Nemotron on Nebius.

## Maya

Had coffee with Maya today. Maya’s birthday is June 12, and she loves hiking and ceramics. I promised Maya I would send her the trail guide.

## Stephen

Stephen is training for his first half marathon. Stephen’s birthday is November 8. I promised Stephen I would check in about his training next week.

## Drake

Drake enjoys jazz. Drake’s birthday is April 8.

## A later correction

Drake told me his birthday is April 18, not April 8 as I had written before. Drake is learning to play piano.

Weaver automatically updates Drake’s birthday from the later note.
The old memory remains in the profile’s previous memories, with its original source.
